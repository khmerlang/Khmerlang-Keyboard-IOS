## 3. Description (4000 chars max)

**English:**

> Khmerlang Keyboard is a modern keyboard for typing Khmer and English on iPhone and iPad — with smart word suggestions, spelling correction, and Roman-to-Khmer input built in.
>
> Everything important happens on your device: suggestions, corrections, and word prediction work offline, with no account and no sign-in.
>
> TYPE KHMER YOUR WAY
> • Full Khmer keyboard layout with long-press secondary characters
> • Roman-to-Khmer input: type "somleng" and get សំឡេង — like a pinyin keyboard for Khmer
> • Optional "space converts to Khmer" mode: press space and your roman word becomes Khmer automatically
> • One-tap ⇄ switch between Khmer and English layouts
> • Khmer and Latin numbers and symbols (១២៣ / ?123)
>
> SMART SUGGESTIONS — ON DEVICE
> • Next-word prediction powered by a bundled Khmer language model
> • Fuzzy spelling correction that understands common Khmer typing mistakes
> • Khmer word segmentation that understands text written without spaces
> • English suggestions and corrections when typing in English
> • Custom dictionary: add your own words and Roman-to-Khmer mappings
>
> PRIVACY FIRST
> • Typing is processed locally on your device by default
> • No account, no sign-in, no tracking
> • Optional cloud spell check is OFF by default and only runs with your explicit consent — you can turn it off anytime
> • Works fully offline without Full Access
>
> EASY SETUP
> 1. Open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards
> 2. Tap "Add New Keyboard…" and choose Khmerlang
> 3. In any app, tap the 🌐 globe key to switch to Khmerlang
>
> Khmerlang Keyboard is built for everyone who types Khmer every day — students, professionals, and families staying in touch. Whether you learned to type on a Khmer layout or you're more comfortable spelling Khmer words with Latin letters, Khmerlang keeps up with you.
>
> Questions or feedback? Visit khmerlang.com or write to us — we read everything.
